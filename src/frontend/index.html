EHR Transcription Safety Gate Research prototype · clinician review required

Clinical documentation quality assurance

Review AI-generated clinical text before it enters the record.

The prototype extracts clinical entities, retrieves terminology evidence, and returns contextual review signals for a human to assess.

Prototype limitation
Not for clinical use. Findings are not diagnoses or treatment recommendations, and the tool does not update an EHR.

Research context · external study

Automated auditing of emergency department documentation

Dietrich N, Stubbert B · The American Journal of Emergency Medicine, 2026

93.2–94.2% reported accuracy across four evaluated LLMs

The study evaluated 600 ED discharge notes: 500 with one injected documentation error and 100 error-free controls. Its results are from that study and are not measurements of this prototype.

System overview
How the review works

      

      

        
1. Extract
Stage A identifies candidate diagnoses, medications, and dosage mentions.

        
2. Retrieve
Medication lookups use RxNorm; diagnosis candidates use a local ICD-10-CM text index. Retrieved evidence is attached to relevant entities.

        
3. Contextual review
In llm_rag mode, Gemini receives the candidate transcript, extracted entities, and retrieved evidence. This is retrieval-augmented prompting—not embedding or vector-database search.

      

      
Evaluation status: the research result above belongs to its authors. This prototype has not been clinically validated or benchmarked to that study.
